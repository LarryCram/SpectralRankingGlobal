# Inventory and Collation of ERA 2018 Materials

This document collates and catalogues all files relating to the **Excellence in Research for Australia (ERA) 2018** national evaluation exercise discovered across the system (including `/home/lc`, `/media/d-drive`, `/media/k-drive`, `/media/m-drive`, and `/media/s-drive`).

It encompasses the regulatory and technical documentation developed during the preparatory years (**2016–2017**), the official **2018** reporting and outcome datasets, the evaluated research output collections spanning the **2011–2016** reference window, and retrospective analytical reviews.

---
